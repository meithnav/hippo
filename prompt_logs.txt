1. Naive prompt(baseline)

```
Generate a STORY for a 5 year old. 

USER INPUT: {user_query}

Structure the story with a clear beginning, middle, and end. 

```

Problems: 
- limited diversity
- less reliable: fail to maintain coherence between paragraphs, adherence to user-query
- subject to halluciantion
- lack of character development
- need to add contraints suitable to our target audience 

--------------------------------------
2. Added:
(a) Destructure the prompts for better understanding (adherence)
(b) Add a planning section (coherence) 
(c) Output format (adherence)
(d) Precautionary contraints 

```
##TASK:## 
Generate a STORY for a **{age} year old** 

##USER QUERY:## 
{user_query}


##PLANNING:##
- Break down the USER QUERY to understand the actors, their relations, plot, genre. 
- Structure the story with a clear beginning, middle, and end. 
- Create dynamic and relatable characters that evolve throughout the story
- Ensure the story is well-balanced, mixing action, dialogue, and description to maintain interest and momentum.


##CONSTRAINTS:## 
- Story is for 5-10 years old.
- Length - Around 4000 - 5000 words. 
- Simple and easy to follow.
- Prevent harmful, foul, horrendous, and lewd language.
- Positive themes and gentle humor only.
- Include a light moral or uplifting lesson.

##OUTPUT FORMAT:## 
1. Give a title
2. Story 
3. Moral 


Be VERBOSE. Be CREATIVE. 
```


Problems: 
- Structure Planning Phase: LLMs follow auto-regressive decoding, components in the Planning phase need to be ordered so that geenration of previous tokens influence the next tokens aptly.

3. Ordering the components of PLANNING

```
##TASK:## 
Generate a STORY for a **{age} year old** 

##USER QUERY:## 
{user_query}


##PLANNING:##
- Break down the USER QUERY to understand the mentioned actors, their relations, plot, genre. 
- Then create dynamic and relatable characters that evolve throughout the story. 
- Develop character personas, relatinships, and relatable discriptions.  
- Chapter Outlines: Structure the story with a clear introduction -> plot setting -> problem ->  difficulties/opportunties -> climax -> ending.  
- Ensure the story is well-balanced, mixing action, dialogue, and description to maintain interest and momentum.
- Weave in gentle humor and a light moral


##CONSTRAINTS:## 
- Story is for 5-10 years old.
- Length - Around 8000 - 10000 words. 
- Simple and easy to follow.
- Prevent harmful, foul, horrendous, and lewd language.
- Positive themes and gentle humor only.
- Tone: Uplifting, with a clear, gentle lesson.

##OUTPUT FORMAT:## 
1. Give a title
2. Story 
3. Moral 

```


Problem: 
- Symbolic Chapter Outlines makes it static dampening creativity in story plots.
- Morals generated through the LLMs is not great. Maintain the pool of morals, randomly pick 1 moral and pass it into our prompt.
- too short story. ~300-500 words


Sugestions: 
- Introduce our custom functions to produce these meta-data instead of relying on LLM. Offers more controllability and reduces inference burden on the LLM.

6. Does few shot examples help? 

- Make a dictionary of stories for each genres. 
- Why dictionary? So that the shared example is not a negative sample for the generation. (Avoid Genre mismatch)

Cons: 
- Increases cost since stories can have ~5-8k tokens
- LLM draws inspiration from the example. limits creativity.
